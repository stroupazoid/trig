Five key points over one cycle

Point12345

For y = A sin(Bx − C) + D, phase shift h = C/B. This explorer uses B > 0. If A = 0, the graph is constant and has no fundamental period.

Build a periodic model from maximum and minimum

Default example: tide height 5 ft at 4 AM and 13 ft at 10 AM. Assume low-to-high is half a cycle: T = 12 hours. Time t is hours after midnight.

A cosine model starts at a maximum when t equals the phase shift. Equivalent sine models are possible.
