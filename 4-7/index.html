4.7: Inverse Trig Playground

Give it a trig value. It gives you an angle. Try a few, then flip some cards!

Blue arc: allowed output angles. Orange ray: your answer. Every inverse function chooses one angle from its principal range.

Remember: sin⁻¹(x) means arcsin(x), the inverse function. The reciprocal 1/sin(x) is csc(x).

No typing, no timer. Try five cards if you have just a minute.
