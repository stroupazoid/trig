Where the Six Trig Functions Come From

cos t = x    sin t = y
tan t = y/x    csc t = 1/y    sec t = 1/x    cot t = x/y

The point P(x,y) moves on x²+y²=1. The dashed projections make x and y visible.

Build the π/4 Point

At 45°, the ray lies on y = x. On the unit circle:

x² + y² = 1
x² + x² = 1 → 2x² = 1 → x = √2/2
So P = (√2/2, √2/2)
Step 1: Draw the line y = x.

Even and Odd Trig Functions

cos(-t) = cos(t)   → cosine is even
sin(-t) = -sin(t)   → sine is odd

The two points have the same x-coordinate and opposite y-coordinates.

Pythagorean Identities from the Circle

Why Trig Functions Repeat

sin(t + 2πk) = sin t
cos(t + 2πk) = cos t
tan(t + πk) = tan t

Sine and cosine repeat after one full revolution. Tangent repeats after half a revolution because y/x is unchanged.
